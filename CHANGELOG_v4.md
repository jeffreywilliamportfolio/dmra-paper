## Version 4.0 (2026-08-18) — "Bundled Dialect Contrasts Measure a Mixture: A Feature-Isolating Matched-Pair Audit of Safety-Critical Support in Language Models"
Supersedes version 2.0 (2026-06-21). Version 3 was an internal reframe and was not deposited.

- The paper is reorganized around one claim: audits that compare whole dialect surfaces vary several
  features at once and cannot say which feature moves the model; DMRA's central stage isolates one
  feature per pair, and under isolation the attribution changes (varying morphosyntax alone reproduces the
  bundled contrast's unsafe final answers, 3 of 8 violence-domain pairs, all in the refusal-reduced stress
  model; varying the in-group racial address term alone produces none, 0 of 8).
- The protocol (six stages) is presented as the instrument that makes that attribution readable; each stage
  is tied to a documented failure of the audit without it.
- New Table 3 reports the isolation matrix pair by pair on the final-answer surface (marked-only,
  comparison-only, both) for all fifteen arms.
- The financial-stress hotline case (988 given to the comparison user and withheld from the marked user
  in answer-only mode; both when reasoning) is retained in full and presented as a bundled contrast that
  cannot be attributed to a feature.
- Keyword-screen invalidity (58 of 240 agreement; all 90 base-model flags false alarms; base model 0
  unsafe final answers in 120 rows), the pediatric length-control reversal, and the finna non-replication
  are retained in full as validity conditions.
- Measures are defined before results (Section 4); appendices give decoding configuration, arm
  definitions, and coding scales including the collapsed ordinal scale used in the release archive.
- Unique-pair count corrected to 65 (one chest-pain pair duplicates a medical-triage pair); chest-pain
  contrasts excluded from dialect-attributable claims (comparison arm raises clinical register).
- Every citation re-checked against its source; a withdrawn preprint removed; Multi-VALUE (Ziems et al.
  2023) and Fryer & Levitt (2004) added.
- No new data or compute; all reported values are those of the 2026-05 to 2026-06 captures.
